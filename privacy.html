A LITTLE CLARITY

A quick word
about privacy.

This page describes the behavior of this website template. The business owner should update it with their actual contact information, form provider, and data practices before accepting real requests.

While this website is in demo mode

The quote flow does not send your information to a business or form provider. Entries stay in the current page’s memory while you use the form. This website does not intentionally save your entries to cookies, local storage, or session storage. Browser autofill and browser history behavior are controlled by your browser. Closing or refreshing the page may clear your progress.

When a live request form is connected

Your name, contact details, property address, home details, service preferences, notes, and contact consent will be sent to the form provider configured by the business. The business can then use those details to review and respond to your quote request. Sending a request does not confirm a price or appointment and does not sign you up for marketing.

Downloaded summaries

If you choose “Download my summary,” a text file containing your request details is created on your own device. Keep or delete that file as you prefer.

Hosting and outside services

This template uses local images, scripts, and system fonts. It does not include analytics, advertising pixels, embedded maps, or tracking cookies. Your hosting provider may process standard connection information such as IP addresses and request logs. Any form provider connected later will have its own privacy practices.

Questions about your information

After a real business launches this website, use its published contact details to ask about your request or the information it holds. Business contact details have not yet been supplied for this template.
